Who we are

Built around the details.

PROJECT: Western Sydney is the development community behind Mount Druitt, an ambitious Australian experience currently being developed on the Roblox platform.

Our focus is on delivering an experience that reflects the detail, atmosphere and realism of Western Sydney. Every aspect of development is approached with a commitment to quality, precision and high standards. We believe that realism is found in the details.

Make it your own

More than just a place.

A free-roam roleplay experience shaped by the people who play it. Choose your path and make your mark.

01 / COMMUNITY

Motorcycle clubs

Build your own club, bring people together and organise rides. Motorcycles, including Harley-Davidsons, are part of the world.

02 / EMERGENCY SERVICES

Serve the community

Take on roles inspired by local police, paramedics and firefighters.

03 / LOCAL WORK

Find a job

Explore local employment opportunities inspired by familiar Australian retailers and workplaces.

04 / DELIVERIES

Out on the road

Pick up delivery work inspired by services such as Uber Eats and explore the streets as you go.

The setting

Inspired by Western Sydney.

The first focus is Mount Druitt, with attention to its streets, landmarks and everyday atmosphere.

Main development focus

Mount Druitt, NSW

The main map is being developed first, with a focus on recreating the local area and its character.

Possible future expansions

Further across the district

Areas such as Emerton, Blackett, Bidwill and Tregear have been considered as possibilities. These expansions are not confirmed.
